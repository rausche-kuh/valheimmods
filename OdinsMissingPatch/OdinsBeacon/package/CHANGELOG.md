## Unreleased

- First release on its own. This was the player marks tweak of Odin's Missing Patch, which is now
  a pack of smaller mods: every other player gets a small glowing mark over their head once they
  are out of sight or far away, and on the edge of the screen when they are off it, so you can
  always tell which way your friends are.
- Your settings from Odin's Missing Patch are carried over on the first start.
