# Changelog

## Unreleased

- First release. Hold `Y` and your character rises into the Forsaken power pose, wreathed in its
  fire, while every item lying around flies to your feet — from 10m after a second, and further
  the longer you hold. It stops by itself once the last item in reach has landed. What fits in
  your backpack is picked up, the rest piles up at your feet. It only works while nothing hostile
  is near or after you, and an item that was moved once (pulled, or dropped by a player) is never
  pulled again, so it tidies up after a fight or a felled forest but never carries a load for
  you. No cooldown, no stamina.
