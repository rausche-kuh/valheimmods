# Roadmap

What the family as a whole still needs. Features belong to a member and go in its own
`ROADMAP.md`; a new idea that fits no member waits here until it gets one.

## Before the first release of the split

- **Icons:** every new member ships Odin's Missing Patch's `package/icon.png` as a placeholder.
- **Play test:** each member on its own and all together, a first start beside an old
  `rauschekuh.odinsmissingpatch.cfg` (settings carried over), and the pack update in Gale on a
  profile that had 0.3.2.
- **Verify:** `Localization.LoadCSV` adds to the translation table rather than replacing it,
  now that several members each load their own CSV (see `docs/translations.md`).
