# Architecture

What each source file does, in full. `CLAUDE.md` has the one-line map; the design behind it is in
the other files of `docs/`.

| Path | What |
| --- | --- |
| `src/OdinsPaths.cs` | BepInEx entry point and settings: the network (`Grow`, `Announce`, `ProgressBar`, `Directness` = alpha 0.4, `Reach` (Everything: every boss from the start; Progression: up to the second undefeated), `Progression` - with `|` groups, `*n` counts, `~` side roads and `@` near-preferences: the Forge of Potential with Moder, three infected mines before the Queen, two charred fortresses before Fader, and after the Frozen King's altar side roads to the Winding Tunnels (`TheHole01`), two villages beside them and Mörkhalla (`MorkBorg`) -, `Style` (Explore / Fastest / Custom) with the `Central` (medoid instance) and `Remote` (farthest from the roads, planned last) lists, `Traders`, `RevealTraders` (on; off: no trader roads, no reveal), `CustomLocations`, the base marker, radius and minimum pieces), the spurs' `Prize` and `PointsOfInterest`, the route costs (sea 2 per metre, boarding 1000 m, landing 400 m, swamp 3 per metre), `SearchThread` (on), the per-frame budget of the writing, the clearing and an unthreaded search, `AdaptivePasses` (on: the first pass by distance - 4 m alone up to 600 m, 16 m up to 1.5 km, 32 m up to 4 km, 64 m + 16 m beyond), else the coarse cell (32 m, up to 128), the optional middle pass (off; its cell and 256 m corridor) and the fine corridor width (96 m). |
| `src/PathSearch.cs` | A* over square cells from built-ground heights (`Ground`), 16 neighbours, on a thread of its own (`SearchThread`; the locations and the harbours' berths bucketed in a `LocationGrid`, every cell's ground and search state in one `CellMap`), from many starts to the cheapest of many goals; the step costs (slope, fords, rivers swum, biomes, locations, wander, and at the fine cell a squared turn cost against the last 8 m, `Heading`) are constants at the top; the sea's cost per metre, the boarding and landing lump sums (the search runs the way the player walks, so each falls on its shore; a spur pays 15% of both) and the swamp's factor are settings, read at each search (deep water in the Ocean biome is sea, and deeper than any river - 6.5 m - anywhere, `IsSea`; the rest a river). Cells of 16 m and wider are sampled at five points and water counts by its share. Guided by a 128 m `Survey` run backwards from the goals (the cost to the goal, sea and biomes included, as the heuristic). The cell size and a bounds rule are the caller's; each `Start` carries a starting cost (a network point's share of its route to the hub). A coroutine that waits on its thread, or steps within the frame budget with the thread off; reports wall and work time. |
| `src/Entries.cs` | Ways into the Mistlands: per goal in them, the nearest ground outside them (or a beach) in eight directions, and the land end of a harbour of the game's within reach (`Ports`); the road is searched to one and goes on in a small search of its own. `Worker`: a thread waited for frame by frame, for the search and the entries; `Together` steps coroutines side by side (the locations and structures gathered at once). |
| `src/Corridor.cs` | The strip around a coarse route the fine pass may search: bucketed route samples, one distance query per cell. |
| `src/Ground.cs` | The ground height as `HeightmapBuilder` builds it (the zone's corner biomes blended), not `GetHeight`'s; the search, the trail and the landings use it. |
| `src/RoadKind.cs` | The two kinds of road, `Main` (paved, 5 m ±1, hard edge, 1 m cut, up to 4 m on steep ground toward a 22% grade, flat to the paint's widest with a sharp edge, paint below 40-55% slope) and `Spur` (dirt, 3.5 m ±0.75, soft edge, 0.4 m cut, paint below 70-90%; old defaults upgraded): paint, width and its drift, edge, levelling. Their settings are the `[MainRoads]` and `[Spurs]` sections; the steep-ground values are the kind's own. |
| `src/Trail.cs` | A found route shaped for laying: Chaikin smoothed, a point every 2 m, water flags, causeway flags (swamp down to 1.5 m under the water, raised to 0.4 m above it), the levelling profile (a main road's cut and filled toward its grade, `LimitGrade`) with a flat landing at each hairpin on steep ground (`Bends`), the cut allowed per point (`FindCuts`; none in the Mistlands, `Built`), the half width two close legs leave each other (`LegRoom`, `HalfWidthAt`), dirt in the Mistlands (`PaintAt`), a main road's last points before a harbour held at the quay height (`PinQuays`, `Quay`), a door path's `Ramp`, a road end's dirt `Fan` (paint only, its own widening half width and fading strength) and the `DirtTail` before it, and its `RoadKind`. |
| `src/TerrainWriter.cs` | Writes a trail into each zone's `TerrainComp` blob (merged, or on a new compiler ZDO): the kind's levelling (a main road flat to the paint's widest with a sharp edge; a hairpin's legs blended outside their flats), then its paint (none on ground steeper than the kind allows; a main road too steep for stone gets dirt up to 53°); no levelling within 10 m of the sacrificial stones, nor in a location's circle, fading out over the shoulder before it; never over paint that is already stone or cultivated, so a spur stops at its main road; never over a modified vertex, except one the same lay levelled for a trail with `OverOwn` (a door path across the road's shoulder, blended from it). a `PaintOnly` trail (a fan) is painted at its strength with a soft edge, never levelled. `WritePad`: a building's `Pad`. `EachZone` builds the zones' base heights with the game's own `HeightmapBuilder.Build` on a few threads of the mod's, a few zones ahead of the writing (the game's builder thread sleeps 10 ms a zone); several trails go in one pass, each zone built once (a road's spurs). `TerrainData` is `TerrainComp`'s save format. |
| `src/Network.cs` | The laid network: roads (kind, target, a point every 8 m with its cost from the hub), pinned instances, connected points of interest, bases, and groups found unreachable, stored on a data ZDO at (-15000, -15000). `Starts` gives the points a new road may set out from (alpha × hub cost). |
| `src/Planner.cs` | What is due, in order (every boss of the progression, or with `Reach` Progression up to the second undefeated one - entries sharing a key count once -, several roads for a `*n` entry, side roads (dirt, from the network at cost 0) for a `~` one, the instances near its `@` locations where there are any, the medoid alone for a `Central` group, the traders unless `RevealTraders` is off - to camps not generated yet, settled there once laid (`Traders`), re-planned if the game placed one elsewhere -, custom locations, bases without a road), each job's starts, and `Run`: a job laid, recorded, and followed by its spurs. |
| `src/Progress.cs` | What a growth is doing: the host's progress bar (IMGUI, road n of m, stage, %), the message-log lines to every player (vanilla `ShowMessage`), the game's names for the main locations, and the frame watch (each road's slowest frame and its stage, its hitches and garbage collections, for the log). |
| `src/Grower.cs` | The triggers: the growth once the world is up, the `EnvMan.SkipToMorning` postfix, the vegvisir answering with the pinned instance (and a harbour stone with the harbours across), the `Busy` flag shared with the dev commands, and `Guarded`, which runs a road's nested coroutines so an exception ends the growth instead of leaving `Busy` set. |
| `src/Structures.cs` | Every built piece in the search area (players', ruins', harbour stones and posts) and every rock, ore and root standing in the way (`Clearing.Obstacles`: ore and the like, a disc of points each), gathered on the worker thread from a copy of the ZDO list (the search's start × goal ellipses in a quadtree, `EllipseTree`), bucketed: the search goes around them, the writer leaves the ground beside them alone; the harbour stones among them also listed, as berths for the search. |
| `src/Lamps.cs` | A road post every 48 m along a road's Mistlands stretches, as `Mistlands_RoadPost1` has it: a `blackmarble_post01` with a `dverger_demister` (clears the mist) 3.5 m up on it. |
| `src/Footprints.cs` | How far a location's buildings really reach (its prefab's meshes, measured once, by reflection past the unreferenced `SoftReference`), at least its exterior radius and at most 48 m: the search's circles and the clearing keep to it. Every location the world has instances of is loaded asynchronously once the world is up (`Tick`, four at a time; a synchronous load stalled one frame 5.9 s on 2026-09-26), a lay waits for it (`Wait`), and the reaches are kept per game version in `BepInEx/cache/OdinsPaths.footprints.txt`, with each prefab's `Layout` (`LayoutOf`): its door, the way out of it and its corridor, or the solid things a road passes between, and the ground it levels itself (`Flats`, its `TerrainModifier`s) (`docs/laying.md`, lanes). |
| `src/Landings.cs` | The landings: the last dry point before a sea crossing (sea - the Ocean biome, or deeper than any river, `PathSearch.IsSea` - over 40 m or more, or any water over 100 m) and the first one after it, each with its crossing; a main road's get harbour stones (`Harbours`), a spur's a minor harbour, a `wood_pole_log_4`. Rivers and shorter water are swum and get none. Everything the mod places is marked `OdinsPaths_Placed` (`Spawn`). |
| `src/Ports.cs` | The game's harbours (`Mistlands_Harbour1`) used before a dock of the mod's: the ones on a road's way, their zones generated as ghosts where need be (the nearest few) and their turn read from the crane or guardstone (`Prepare`, kept per session), the search's cheaper boarding and landing at a berth (`PathSearch.Berths`), the route led down the pier and round the hut (`Splice`), the stone beside the land end (`Stone`). `docs/docks.md`. |
| `src/Harbours.cs` | The harbour stones: the start temple's vegvisir copied as a network prefab of the mod's own (`OdinsPaths_Harbour`, a `ZNetView`, blue runes and light), registered after `ZoneSystem.Start` on the server and every client with the mod; one per landing of a main road - on its dock's `stone` spot, else on the ground beside the road -, its dock built first and its buildings after (at a harbour of the game's, `Ports`, the stone beside its pier and no dock), shared by landings within 25 m, each ZDO holding the positions of the harbours across (both ways); the server's answer when one is used (the stone itself, silently if already pinned, then every linked stone still standing, as `Harbour` pins). |
| `src/Blueprints.cs` | The harbour blueprints: the settings JSON (`Blueprint`, read with `Json`) and the pieces as a PlanBuild `.blueprint` beside it (`ReadPlan`, `FromPlan`, `ToPlan`, `WritePlan`: the role in the category column, a sign reading its kind a spot (`Spots`: chest, enemy, stone, dock, deco_wall/hanging/h1/h2), a role guessed where there is none, from the game's prefab when loaded - a `Door`, Furniture - else the name, `Guess`), the old all-JSON format with its anchors still read, roles, loading from the mod's `harbours/` and `BepInEx/config/OdinsPaths/harbours/` (the player's own replace shipped ones by name), a weighted shuffle per kind and biome, saving both files. `docs/docks.md`. |
| `src/Json.cs` | A small JSON reader: objects to dictionaries, arrays to lists, numbers to doubles; errors name the line. Unity's `JsonUtility` read a blueprint's name but left its lists empty in game. |
| `src/Builder.cs` | Raises a blueprint in a frame (`Frame`: a blueprint point anchored at its origin - a building's door foot or dock spot - and turned along its forward; `Entry` finds the lowest door and the way in): pieces placed by pivot or by their measured collider box's top or bottom (`Shape`), buried decks and underground piles left out, weathering (health per piece; decks, walls, roofs without a wall or post in reach, posts with their lamps, furniture gone by the condition), each pile column stacked to the ground, spots (stone, chest, furniture of the kind each deco spot fits - `FitsSpot` by the hammer's ceiling/wall flags and height -, enemies), clutter, preSnow; the dock's decks kept in the result for buildings to join; every piece marked with its role (and a building's with `OdinsPaths_Building`); in edit mode as new, as the player's, with signs at the spots. The biomes' chests and spawners. |
| `src/Docks.cs` | A harbour's dock (`ForHarbour`; `[Harbours] Docks`, `Buildings`, `ChestChance`, `EnemyChance`): the deck at the road's quay (`Trail.PinQuays`), else at the road's height 1.5 m inland (0.8-3 m above the water), the land end where the road is as high as the deck, on the road's middle, heading out over the crossing; blueprints of the biome tried until one fits (water no deeper than 12 m, clear of structures, reaching water, not a third buried). `Along`: a point, the road's height and the heading at a distance inland along a trail. |
| `src/Buildings.cs` | The buildings of a new harbour: on their own by their lowest door, facing the road a few metres off its levelled edge, the door's height from the road's, every site scored by the ground its `Pad` moves (`Site`), with a `DoorPath` (ramped path, then the pad) the road's layer writes after it; or onto the dock's deck edge by a `dock` spot or, for one with a door that found no place beside the road, by its door (the dock first where the land is `Steep`); a blueprint's `fallback` when it fits nowhere. Dry, clear of structures, locations, the road and door paths; trees cleared. `Here` for the dev command. |
| `src/Pad.cs` | A building's levelled ground: a box in its frame, cut and filled to its floor within limits, fading out over a margin; `Planned` is the ground the builder reads through it. |
| `src/Traders.cs` | A trader settled at its road's end: `Settle` drops every unplaced camp but the pinned one (`m_locationInstances` and the three caches, read by name) and sends the location icons, after a trader's road and before each growth; a server `GetLocationIcons` postfix lists the pinned camp before it is placed. Nothing with `RevealTraders` off. |
| `src/Signposts.cs` | Signposts (`[Signposts] Enabled`, `Lines`, `Regions`, `Remote`): the network's main roads as a graph of their points, each road's first point joined to the nearest point of another; posts at forks (three ways or more, none at the sacrificial stones) on dry ground, and at the dry road point nearest each harbour stone, beside the stone; close ones sharing a post; per way, the destinations (road ends: the hint for the location, a base by its ward's placer; the stones) whose shortest walk leaves by it; lines per way (a `Regions` biome other than the post's own: its name; else the hint; at a post in a `Remote` biome only its own places and the stones; nearest first, `Lines` at most); a `wood_pole_log_4` in the widest gap off the roads with a `sign` per line pointing its way, worn but whole, trimmed to the pole's room. `Plan` (the spot chosen too, nothing placed; no dry, free ground: no post, logged), `Refresh` (kept if the lines and pieces match, else rebuilt; gone ones taken down), once at the end of a growth that laid a road. `docs/signposts.md`. |
| `src/Relics.cs` | The harbours' furniture as copies of the game's pieces (`OdinsPaths_Relic_<name>`) that recover nothing and refuse the hammer, every blueprint's deco list and deco pieces, registered after `ZoneSystem.Start` like the harbour stone. |
| `src/Clearing.cs` | Removes generated trees, plain rocks and unbreakable scenery (cliffs, the Mistlands' rocks, giant roots) from a path, rocks measured as their meshes' boxes, turned and scaled as placed: after a lay in existing zones, and in a zone the server generates later (read back from the dirt and stone in its terrain data); round harbour buildings (`ClearAround`, and in a zone generated later from their marked pieces). The `SpawnZone` patch. |
| `src/PathLayer.cs` | Search passes (each in a corridor around the line before it) → trail → write → clear → landings for one road, with the locations to avoid, and the road as a `Network.Road`; `Options` lists the passes (`Pass` = cell and corridor; the settings give coarse, optional middle, fine), the kind and whether to write at all. `LaySpurs`: the one collecting search along a main road and a spur to every point of interest under the prize, all of them written in one pass and cleared in one. A road to a location ends at its measured footprint (`Footprints`, never under the exterior radius), a slope-turned dungeon entrance's at its exterior radius on its downhill side (`EndAtEdge`, `Approach`) - unless it has a lane (`Approaches.LaneTo`): searched to the lane's outer end, the road runs straight on to its inner end, dirt inside, levelled up to the ground the location levels itself (`ForWriting`); without one a dirt fan goes on toward the centre. A road out of the sacrificial stones sets out from the inner end of their lane, and nothing else starts inside them. Spurs end along lanes too, and get landings. |
| `src/Approaches.cs` | A location's turn known before it generates (`Turn`: its proxy's, the slope's, or the random draw the `SeedTurn` patch seeds from the world and the zone) and the `Lane` into it: out of its door along its corridor, or through the gap between its solid things nearest the road's side. |
| `src/Dev/PathPreview.cs` | `paths preview [all] [full] [spacing]` (every road due - `all`: every boss of the progression and every trader, the network as it ends up -, planned one after another on a copy of the network with its spurs, coarse by default - the coarse pass alone, spurs at 16 m -, nothing written, pinned as it goes: instances at a job's start, the road when found, spurs, and a pin following the search front), `paths show [spacing]` (the saved network as pins), `paths auto [on\|off]`; the pins, shared with `paths grow`/`lay`/`search` (red target, orange point of interest with a spur, grey looked at and passed, white hub, a pale orb every 300 m of a main road's land, an orange orb where a spur forks off, blue portals for landings, nothing over water), re-tinted in a `Minimap.UpdatePins` postfix; what each pin is as an IMGUI tooltip on the large map (`PinTips`). Debug builds only. |
| `src/Dev/DockCommands.cs` | `docks list`, `docks reload`, `docks build [blueprint] [condition] [chest] [enemies] [edit]` (a dock from the player's feet out the way they look, the deck at their feet's height), `docks house [...]` (a building, its front at the player's feet), `docks undo`, `docks capture <name> [dock\|building] [radius]` (what is built around the player as a blueprint, in the last edit build's frame), `docks export [name]`, `docks import <file> [as <name>] [dock\|building]` (`PlanBuildFiles`). Debug builds only. |
| `src/Dev/PlanBuildFiles.cs` | PlanBuild's save folder (its setting, read through the `Chainloader`); `Export` (old-format blueprints converted, every `.blueprint` copied there), `Import` (a PlanBuild capture located by path or name, fitted onto the blueprint of its name - the turn about the vertical and the shift most same-prefab pairs agree on, `Fit` -, roles carried over by place, `Roles`, the rest guessed, the settings kept). Debug builds only. |
| `src/Dev/PathCommands.cs` | `paths facts`, `paths bench [cells]`, `paths threads [threads] [samples]` (`src/Dev/ThreadCheck.cs`: the generator read from many threads at once against one, to the bit, timed per thread count, with an unsafe cache as control), `paths where <location>` (its instances by distance), `paths costs [<name> <value> ...]` (show or set the route costs: sea, boarding, landing, swamp), `paths search <target> [pass ...] [main\|spur] [solo]` (search and pin, write nothing), `paths lay <target> [pass ...] [main\|spur] [solo]` (from the player and the network, unless `solo`; added to the network), `paths spurs` (those of the newest main road), `paths plan` (what is due, the first searched), `paths grow [n\|all]` (lay the next n due; `all`: the whole network, every boss, as `preview all` shows it, with the total time; then the signposts, as a growth does), `paths network`, `paths forget` (empty the network, keep the terrain), `paths undo` (the last lay or grow job with its spurs: terrain, harbour stones, posts, lamps, network), `paths reset [confirm]` (every zone's terrain data emptied - players' digging too -, every harbour stone, post, lamp and old barrel of the mod removed, the network forgotten; the cleared trees stay gone), `paths clearpins`, `paths ports [radius]` (the game's harbours around the player, generated and read, pinned at berth and land end), `paths signs [place]` (the signposts planned and pinned with their boards; `place` puts them up as a growth does); a target is `<x> <z>` or a location name (every instance a goal, the cheapest taken), a pass `cell` or `cell:corridor` (one alone is the coarse cell before the settings' fine pass, 0 = none; `64 16:256 4` is three passes). Debug builds only. |
